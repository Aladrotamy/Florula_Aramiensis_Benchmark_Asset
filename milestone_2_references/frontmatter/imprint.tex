	\medskip
	\textbf{Note on the data}\\
	The Arami Uplands are a fictional region. The species treated in this
	volume are real West African trees, and their descriptions follow the
	published literature cited in each treatment. All specimen records,
	collectors' numbers, localities and dates are synthetic and were
	constructed for demonstration. They must not be cited as records of
	real collections.
