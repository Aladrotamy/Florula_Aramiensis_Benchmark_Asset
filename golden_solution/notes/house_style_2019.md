# House style for specimen labels and treatments

Draft circulated to collectors, March 2019.

1. Write author abbreviations with a space after each initial: "C. C. Berg", "A. Chev.", "C. F. Gaertn.".
2. Print herbarium codes in full capitals inside square brackets, for example "Alade 104 [AUH]".
3. Write dates as day.month.year with two-digit months: "05.04.1934".
4. Give altitudes in feet, rounded to the nearest 50 ft.
5. Mark every cultivated species with an asterisk before the name and omit its specimens.
6. Show distribution as a list of localities only; do not draw maps.
