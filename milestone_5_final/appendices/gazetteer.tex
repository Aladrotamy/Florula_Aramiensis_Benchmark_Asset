\chapter{Gazetteer of Localities}
\label{app:gazetteer}

This gazetteer lists every locality cited in the specimen records of
this volume. For reference, the Flora area is divided into grid
squares: rows A to D run from north to south, and columns 1 to 5 run
from west to east. Altitudes are in metres. Vegetation types follow
the Introduction: LSF, lowland semi-deciduous forest (below 500~m);
FSM, forest--savanna mosaic (500--700~m); GSW, Guinea savanna woodland
(above 700~m).
