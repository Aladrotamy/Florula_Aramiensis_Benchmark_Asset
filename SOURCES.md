# Sources, Licences and Originality

This file answers the hard requirement that no copyright-licensed content was used to produce the asset and that every external resource has a source URL.

## 1. What is original to this asset

All of the following were written for this asset and reproduce no third-party text, images or data:

- every LaTeX source file (`main.tex`, `florula.sty`, front matter, chapters, keys, family treatments, appendices);
- the house style, the identification keys and the distribution-map macro;
- all data in `data/` (species checklist, localities, specimens, phenology), the notes and the build files;
- all scripts in `tools/` and `verifier/`, and all documentation.

The Arami Uplands, their localities, collectors, herbaria and all 90 specimen records are fictional. The specimen data are generated by `tools/make_specimens.py` (seed 20260927).

## 2. How published sources were used

The species descriptions, uses, synonyms and author citations are written in the asset's own words from general botanical knowledge and are **cited**, not copied, to the works below. No passage, table, figure, photograph, key or map from any of these works is reproduced. Scientific names, author abbreviations and bibliographic citations are facts and are not subject to copyright.
