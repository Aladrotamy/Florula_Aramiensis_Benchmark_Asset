# Asset Metadata

## Asset
Florula Aramiensis Benchmark Asset

## Owner
Rotimi Alade

## Domain
Overleaf / LaTeX (academic and scholarly publishing)

## Project
*Florula Aramiensis, Volume I: Economic Trees*: a scholarly regional flora of the Arami Uplands, a fictional West African region, treating 18 real West African tree species in 8 families.

## Format
Multi-file LaTeX book (`book` class) with a custom house style (`florula.sty`), compiled with pdfLaTeX and BibTeX (natbib, plainnat). 44 pages. 31 source files in 11 folders, of five artifact types: LaTeX source, BibTeX, tab-separated data, Markdown notes, and JSON/YAML build files.

## Data
Fully synthetic specimen data, regenerated byte-for-byte by `tools/make_specimens.py` (seed 20260927): 90 specimens, 18 species, 12 localities, 3 collectors in three periods (1934–1938, 1961–1967, 2019–2025), 3 herbaria. Species descriptions, uses, synonyms and author citations follow published literature (15 references). Every specimen satisfies six documented data rules.
